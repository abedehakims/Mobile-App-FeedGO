# FeedGO – Mobile Application

**FeedGO** is a mobile application designed to simplify food business management through product management, profit margin simulation, and inventory monitoring.

The application helps business owners manage their product menus, estimate profitability, and monitor stock availability in one place.

## ✨ Features

### 1. Profit Margin Simulation
- Calculate product selling prices and profit margins.
- Simulate potential profit based on product costs and selling prices.
- View detailed margin calculations to support better business decisions.

### 2. Product Management
- Add new products or menu items.
- Update existing product information.
- Manage product data in an organized way.

### 3. Stock Monitoring
- Monitor product stock availability.
- Keep track of inventory information.
- Simplify stock management for daily business operations.
